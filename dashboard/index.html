    About the backtest

    The HMM set sector equity exposure. XGBoost ranked the sector ETFs, and the original strategy invested the rest in TLT.

    The saved benchmark compounds equal-weight sector returns. The SPY line uses adjusted closes on the same dates. Sharpe and Sortino use 13 four-week periods a year. Drawdown uses the saved four-week marks.

    The original HMM could use later observations inside each prediction block. The ranker also allowed a training label to reach the first test date. The chart is a historical record of that run. Saved regimes and simulated fills begin May 17, 2024.
